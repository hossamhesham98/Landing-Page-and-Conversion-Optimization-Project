# Docloroo

## Project Overview

Docloroo is a lifestyle brand offering a creative coloring blanket that combines warmth, entertainment, and shared experiences. Designed for children, young women, friends, and couples, the product transforms an everyday blanket into an interactive activity that encourages creativity and quality time, especially during the winter season.

## Project Objective

To establish Docloroo as a distinctive lifestyle brand within the creative gifting and Craft market by building brand awareness, communicating its unique product value, and generating initial customer demand and sales. The brand aims to position the product as both a fun personal experience and a thoughtful gift for different occasions.

## Project Scope

### Positioning Statement

Docloroo is a creative lifestyle brand that transforms an ordinary blanket into an engaging coloring experience, combining cozy comfort with creative play, unlike traditional blankets or standalone coloring activities, Docloroo brings both together in one product, making everyday moments more playful and enjoyable for children and everyone uses it.

### Unique Value Proposition

Docloroo is more than a blanket. It's a shared experience combines the comfort of a blanket with the fun of coloring and personalizing it with the people you love, it gives children a creative activity they can enjoy while staying cozy, and offering women a playful way to enjoy creative time on their own or with friends, every time you use it, you're not just wrapping yourself in comfort, you're wrapping yourself in the memories, laughter, and happiness created together. ✨
